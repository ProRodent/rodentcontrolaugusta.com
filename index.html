Know Your Pest

Norway Rat vs. Roof Rat vs. House Mouse

Augusta is home to all three. Correct ID changes everything about the treatment plan, the wrong approach wastes money and leaves the colony intact.

SpeciesWhere FoundEntry PointsPeak SeasonMain Treatment
Norway Rat
Rattus norvegicus		 Basements, sewers, restaurant dumpsters, ground-level burrows along the Savannah River strip Foundation gaps, utility holes, garage doors Year-round in Augusta's subtropical climate Bait stations + ground-level exclusion sealing
Roof Rat
Rattus rattus		 Attics, soffits, wall voids, travels live-oak canopy in Summerville, Forest Hills, Highland Park Roofline gaps, soffit vents, tree-branch overhangs Fall-Winter peak, active year-round Attic trapping + roofline exclusion + tree trimming
House Mouse
Mus musculus		 Kitchens, pantries, wall cavities, any frame, any Augusta area Gaps as small as ¼ inch, door sweeps, pipe gaps Year-round, indoor populations spike in fall Multi-catch traps + micro-gap sealing

Common Questions

Augusta Rodent Control FAQ

What rodents are most common in Augusta, GA?

Augusta has three main pest rodents: Norway rats along the Savannah River belt and restaurant districts. Roof rats in attics throughout Summerville, Forest Hills. Highland Park (where the live-oak canopy creates above pathways); and house mice year-round in kitchens and wall cavities across every area. Our subtropical climate means all three are active every month of the year.

Do you offer same-day rodent removal in Augusta?

Yes. We dispatch 24/7 and most often have same-day site visit slots across Richmond County. Call (844) 635-0403 and we'll confirm same-day availability for your certain handle and zip code.

How much does rodent control cost in Augusta?

The quote depends on home size, the species involved, and how many entry points need sealing. Attic cleanup and insulation replacement are separate scopes. We give a free written quote after the on-site inspection, and we do not quote figures over the phone.

Are your treatments safe for pets and children?

Yes. We use tamper-resistant bait stations positioned where pets and children cannot reach them. We favor trapping-plus-exclusion approaches for active home infestations. We walk through every product, putting, and precaution with you on-site before anything is deployed.

Do you serve areas outside Augusta proper?

Yes. We serve all of Richmond County plus Evans, Grovetown, Hephzibah, Blythe, Thomson, Harlem. Waynesboro in Georgia, and North Augusta, Aiken, Graniteville, Edgefield, and several other Aiken and Edgefield County areas on the South Carolina side.

What makes Augusta's rodent issue other from other cities?

Three things set Augusta apart: year-round subtropical breeding (no yearly die-off), the live-oak canopy that gives roof rats above reach to attics across half the city. The Savannah River belt that sustains Norway rat populations near downtown and the Augusta Canal. Add in Masters Week short-term rental turnover and the Fort Eisenhower housing belt and you have truly year-round, multi-species pressure that needs local expertise to manage.
