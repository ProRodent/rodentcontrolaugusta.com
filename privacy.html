Privacy Policy

Useful date: January 1, 2023 · Last updated: January 1, 2026 · Applies to rodentcontrolaugusta.com

Augusta Rodent Control ("we," "us," or "our") runs the website rodentcontrolaugusta.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what details we collect, how we use it. Your rights with respect to that info. This policy applies to residents of Georgia and, where applicable, South Carolina and other states we serve.

1. Details We Collect

Info You Give Voluntarily

Because we run as a call-only business, we do not keep contact forms, newsletter signups, or email capture mechanisms on this Site. The main way you interact with us is by calling (844) 635-0403. When you call, your phone number may be seen to us through standard caller ID. We may record details you offer during the call to book and run services.

If you send us an email at contact@rodentcontrolaugusta.com, we collect your email handle and the content of your message.

Info Collected Automatically

Like most websites, we may on its own collect certain technical info when you visit the Site, with your IP handle, browser type and version, pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring URL. This info is collected through standard server logs and/or analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) and is used in aggregate form to see how visitors use the Site. We do not link this data to your personal identity.

We may use cookies or similar tracking technologies to work core site works and collect analytics data. You can disable cookies through your browser settings; doing so may affect site functionality.

2. How We Use Your Info

We use info collected from calls and email communications to: set rodent control services, follow up on work inquiries, confirm appointments, and offer buyer help. We use on its own collected technical data to improve Site content and results. We do not sell your personal info to third parties.

3. Call Tracking

Our phone number may route through call-tracking software (like Ringba or similar platforms) that records call metadata such as call length, time, and source. This data helps us measure our advertising effectiveness. It may be stored by the call-tracking provider subject to their own privacy policies. We do not use this data to spot or pattern you for marketing goals outside of our direct work relationship.

4. Third-Party Services

Our Site may use third-party services like Google Analytics (analytics), Google Maps (embedded maps on our Contact page). Google Fonts (typography loading). Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing the data they collect when their parts load on our pages. We encourage you to review Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com.

5. Data Retention

We retain call and work records for as long as reasonably must-do to give services and resolve disputes, most often no longer than 36 months after your last work interaction. Analytics data is retained per the settings of the analytics platform we use. You may request deletion of your personal details at any time by contacting us.

6. Your Rights

No matter of your state of residence, you may: request reach to personal info we hold about you. Request correction of inaccurate details; request deletion of your info (subject to legal retention needs); and opt out of any email communications from us. To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@rodentcontrolaugusta.com or call (844) 635-0403.

Georgia residents: Georgia law does not now need a separate privacy notice beyond what is disclosed here. We respect your rights as a Georgia consumer and will respond to privacy requests within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal info from children. If you believe a child has gave us with personal details, please contact us right away.

8. Security

We put in place reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect details collected via this Site, like HTTPS encryption for all page traffic. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot promise sheer security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the Site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
