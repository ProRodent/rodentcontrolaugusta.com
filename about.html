About Augusta Rodent Control

A locally-owned, rodent-focused pest company serving Richmond County and the CSRA since 2023. No generalist pest fluff, just rats, mice, and the expertise to handle both in Augusta's certain setting.

Why We Started, and Why It Matters

Augusta Rodent Control was founded in 2023 because Richmond County needed something that didn't exist: a pest company built around rodents namely, staffed by people who known Augusta's local setting. Ran honestly, no upsells, no phantom reviews, no fabricated certifications.

Most pest companies in the Augusta area treat rodents as one line item in a menu of 30 services. That means the technician handling your Summerville roof-rat infestation yesterday was spraying for termites across town this morning. They're generalists. Rodent work needs something varied, a knowledge of species action, building methods gaps, Augusta's certain climate patterns. The difference between a temporary fix and a lasting one.

The Savannah River belt sustains Norway rat populations that don't fluctuate with season. The Augusta Canal and factory freight zones along the west side keep harborage setting year-round. Fort Eisenhower's military family housing sees tenant turnover that creates inspection gaps. And the live-oak canopy that makes Summerville, Forest Hills. Highland Park good-looking also gives roof rats direct reach to every attic in the part.

These aren't issues that a generalist pest company, handling a dozen service types across three counties, has the time to know deeply. That's why we exist, and why we keep our scope narrow on goal.

Our Running Principles

Six things that don't change no matter of job size, house type, or buyer.

Call-only, always

We don't use contact forms or email leads. Every buyer call goes to a person who can answer questions and book work right now. (844) 635-0403, 24/7.

Species-first diagnosis

Norway rat, roof rat, and house mouse need other treatments. We spot the species before recommending anything, because misidentification leads to wasted money and persistent infestations.

Honest scope

We quote what your home really needs. We don't inflate the scope with add-ons that don't serve your spot. We don't minimize issues to win the first call.

No phantom claims

No invented awards, no fabricated review counts, no unnamed "industry certifications." If a credential isn't real and verifiable, it doesn't appear on this site.

GA rules followed

Georgia structural pest control rules apply to chemical treatments in our service area. We follow them. Any chemical-based work on a site we service complies with state needs.

Exclusion, not just removal

Removing the rats without sealing their entry points is a temporary fix. We suggest and do exclusion work because that's the only thing that makes a treatment last.
