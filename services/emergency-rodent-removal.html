What Qualifies as a Rodent Urgent

Not every rodent setup is an urgent, some are urgent but can be scheduled within a day or two. We use the after framework for triage:

Call Right away (Same-Day Dispatch)

  • Active rat or mouse in a business kitchen, food storage area, or restaurant before work.
  • Dead-rodent odor from inside a wall or ceiling, decomposing carcass that needs same-day site and removal.
  • Rat seen in a living space with infants, toddlers, elderly residents, or immunocompromised people.
  • Daytime rat movement in or around a residence. Norway rats moving in daylight show a large or stressed colony.
  • Rat bites or direct contact with a rat or mouse, seek medical care first, then call us.
  • Business house notified of health department follow-up check needing logged rodent cleanup.

Set up Within 24-48 Hours (Urgent, Not Urgent)

  • Droppings found but no active sighting, confirms presence, doesn't need fast response.
  • Scratching sounds in attic or walls at night, active, but nocturnal rodents running on their normal book.
  • Single mouse sighting in a home with no apparent food access or nesting signs.
  • Pre-listing walkthrough for a home sale, planned, not reactive.
  • Annual prevention visit or exclusion follow-up.

Dead Rodent in Wall. What to Do

Dead-rodent odor from inside a wall cavity is among the most unpleasant household cases Augusta owners find. The odor is distinctive, sweet, heavy, and penetrating. It worsens over several days before improving as decomposition slows. Standard progression: day 1-3, odor begins. Day 3-7, peak odor. Day 10-14, odor begins to fade. Day 14-21, odor largely dissipates. Locating the source before that progression finishes, and removing it, is almost always worth doing.

Locating a dead rodent in a wall cavity needs thermal imaging, probing, or strategic reach to the wall surface. We do this routinely and know the shortcuts (common nesting sites in Augusta wall cavities, the attic drop-down zones where rodents most often die during bait treatment, the behind-pipe spaces that are the most common dead-rat spots in kitchen walls).

If you have dead-rodent odor from a wall, call us same-day, we'll set a place-and-removal visit with priority.

Is It Really an Urgent?: Choice Framework

The word "urgent" gets used loosely in pest control marketing, and there's a real cost to either over- or under-classifying a setup. Treat an issue as urgent when it could wait until Monday and you've spent more than you needed to. Wait until Monday when the setup really needed same-day response and you've allowed health, structural, or business-disruption risk to grow. What to Do Before the Urgent Crew Arrives

If you've by now called and we're en route, a few fast actions on your part materially improve outcomes. None need pest-control expertise, they're about sealing until trained help arrives.

Isolate the affected area. Close doors to rooms where you've seen movement. For restaurants, cordon off the affected food-prep section and log the time of sealing for health-check records. For homes with pets or children, restrict reach to the area until the technician arrives.

Don't try DIY trapping in the rush window. Snap traps placed reactively can create extra sanitation issues if a rodent is caught and not promptly removed. Store-bought rodenticides take days to work, far too slow for a rush spot. Save the DIY for non-urgent prevention work, not active rush calls.

Record for insurance and walkthrough records. If droppings are seen, take photos before any cleaning. Note times of sightings. Restaurant managers should record the time of last health check and any prior pest-control work. We use this info to scope the response and to give records right for whatever regulatory or insurance follow-up applies.

Don't clean up droppings or nesting stuff before we arrive unless it's actively contaminating food prep. Disturbance can spread aerosolized particles and complicates the site visit. Holding first, cleanup as part of the expert response.

Often Asked Questions: Urgent Rodent Removal

What counts as a rodent urgent?

True rush calls needing fast same-day response: active rat or mouse in a business kitchen. Dead-rodent odor from a wall or ceiling. Daytime rat sighting (shows large colony); active rodent in a space with very young children or immunocompromised people. And post-health-walkthrough fix with a deadline. Everything else is urgent but can often be scheduled within 24-48 hours.

How fast can you respond?

We prioritize rush calls in Richmond County and confirm an arrival window on the call. After-hours and overnight calls are dispatched with the same priority as daytime calls. Exact timing depends on current dispatch load and your location within the service area.

Is rush work more expensive?

We confirm the terms with you on the phone before anyone is dispatched, so nothing is a surprise on arrival. Treatment scope is still quoted from what the inspection finds, whatever time the call comes in.

What should I do before you arrive?

Keep people and pets away from the area of rodent signs. Do not try to corner or catch a live rat. Remove or seal exposed food in kitchens. Leave droppings and nesting goods in place, we need to check action levels. If there's dead-rodent odor from a wall, do not try to open the wall yourself.

Do you handle business restaurant rush calls?

Yes. We respond to restaurant, retail, and business kitchen rodent rush calls throughout Augusta, with health-department-tied fix cases. We offer written records of all treatment visits.

What causes daytime rat signs?

Norway rats are normally nocturnal. Daytime signs most often signals one of three things: a very large colony whose nighttime food sources can't feed the whole numbers. A recently disturbed nest from building or heavy rain. Or a sick or dying rat. Any daytime sighting warrants fast expert review.

Is a dead rat in my wall a rush?

It's high-priority. Decomposition odor peaks at days 3-7 and persists 2-4 weeks, attracting secondary pests. Locating and removing a wall-cavity carcass needs pro tools. We prioritize these calls for same-day service when possible.

My health inspector found rodent proof. What do I do?

Call us at once. We prioritize post-walkthrough fix cases and can give same-day treatment with written records for your follow-up with the health department. Business kitchen rodent fix has set standards, expert role is key.

Can rats bite humans?

Yes. Norway rats bite if cornered or handled directly. If bitten, wash the wound thoroughly and seek medical care right away before calling us. Do not try to corner or catch a live rat.
